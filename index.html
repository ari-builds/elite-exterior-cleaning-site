01 — Exterior cleaning, Metro Atlanta & North Georgia A higher standard
Why it matters

Dirt is not the
real problem.

The problem is what the dirt is holding: algae eating into shingles, mildew in the siding, clay packed into concrete, and water finding a gutter that stopped flowing months ago.

Elite Exterior Cleaning is owner-operated by Justin Hall and works across Metro Atlanta & North Georgia. The approach is simple — identify the surface, choose the right pressure and chemistry, protect everything that should not get wet, and do not leave until the edges match the middle.

01Right pressure

Soft wash where it belongs, surface cleaner where it belongs, and never the same tip for both.

02Right chemistry

Dilution matched to the material, so the growth dies without bleaching the finish.

03Right finish

Edges, corners, joints and the walkaround — the parts a fast crew skips.

05 — The process

Four steps,
no mystery.

You know who is coming, what they will use, and what it costs before anyone pulls into the driveway.

  1. 01

    Walkaround

    Fifteen minutes on site. We test a small area, check the surface and the water source, and photograph anything delicate.

  2. 02

    Written quote

    A fixed price for the scope, with the method named. No vague range and no day-of upcharge.

  3. 03

    Protect & clean

    Plants covered and wet down, windows closed, then the right method for each surface — soft wash or surface cleaner.

  4. 04

    Walk it with us

    We walk the property together, fix anything you spot, and leave the before-and-after photos with the invoice.
