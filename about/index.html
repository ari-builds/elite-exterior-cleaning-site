The standard

“A higher standard”
is not a slogan.

It is a list of things that do not get skipped: the test patch, the plants, the edges, the walkaround. Most of it is invisible when the job is done — which is exactly the point.

Justin started Elite to do exterior cleaning properly: soft washing where pressure would cause damage, surface cleaning where it produces an even finish, and a written scope so the price does not move halfway through the day.

The company works with homeowners, realtors, property managers and small commercial sites. Residential and commercial, one property or a recurring schedule. You deal with the owner, and the owner is on site.

What we hold to

Four rules on
every property.

  • 01

    Test before you treat

    A small patch tells us more than a phone description. It is how we avoid bleaching a finish or etching a surface.

  • 02

    Protect the surroundings

    Plants, furniture, pets and neighbours. Water goes where it is supposed to and nowhere else.

  • 03

    Name the method

    The quote says soft wash or pressure wash, and why. You should not have to guess what will happen to your siding.

  • 04

    Walk it before you pay

    The job is finished when you have walked it and anything you spotted has been fixed. Not when the truck pulls away.

Residential & commercial

The same crew.
The same standard.

Homes get the careful version. Commercial properties get the same careful version on a schedule — storefronts, sidewalks, dumpster pads and building washes, early morning or after close.

If you manage multiple properties, ask about a recurring scope. One point of contact, a photo record after each visit, and a calendar that does not slip.
